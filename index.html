Footfall Backup Sync

Footfall Backup Sync is a private, internal tool of b999solutions. It runs on its owner's own computer and copies that computer's backup files to the owner's own Google Drive, then checks that every copy in Drive matches the original byte for byte.

What it does

  • Uploads files from the owner's backup folders on the owner's computer to folders in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Verifies each uploaded file against the original.
  • Writes a short local log of what was copied and whether the check passed.

Who uses it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's account is connected to it.
